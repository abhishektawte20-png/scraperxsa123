ScraperX settings

These apply to every run.

Pacing

The extension runs one query at a time through a real browser tab, at the speed a person would. Shortening these makes Google far more likely to serve a CAPTCHA and stall the run.

Search

Sends your "Not this company if it also mentions" terms to Google as -"term", so the wrong company never takes up a result slot. Only your own typed terms are used, never the auto-detected guesses.

One extra query on the bare company name. If it turns up a second company sharing the name, you settle it once instead of in every boolean. Silent when the name is unambiguous.

A few extra JSON lookups per run, not search queries — GLEIF (LEI index), SEC EDGAR full-text search, and OpenCorporates. Coverage is narrow per source, so this only ever adds corroboration; coming back empty is not treated as a signal either way.

Works with no token at all, capped at 500 requests/month on your own connection. A free token from opencorporates.com raises that.

Free, self-serve key from developer.company-information.service.gov.uk. Skipped entirely without a key — there's no useful anonymous allowance for this one.
